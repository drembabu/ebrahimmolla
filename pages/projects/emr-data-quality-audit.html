From Register to Electronic Medical Records: A Data-Quality Audit

A retrospective audit of orthopedic outpatient records supporting EMR implementation in a resource-limited setting, JAN 2025 - JUN 2025.

Overview

Paper-based registers remain common in resource-limited hospital settings, but poor data completeness and consistency can undermine care quality and health information systems. This study audited orthopedic outpatient records at Khulna Medical College Hospital to build an evidence base for transitioning from paper registers to electronic medical records (EMR).

Approach

I conducted a retrospective audit of 4,085 orthopedic outpatient records, assessing completeness, validity, and internal consistency against standardized data-quality criteria, to quantify the gap between existing register-based recording and the requirements of a reliable EMR system.

My role

I led the EMR transformation effort in the orthopedic outpatient department that motivated this study, including standardizing data templates and staff training, and then designed and conducted the retrospective audit, analysis, and manuscript preparation, improving data completeness from 85% to 97% in the process.
