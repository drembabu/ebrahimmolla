Research · Antimicrobial Resistance

Hidden Co-resistance Modules in Wound Enterobacterales

Network-based evidence from a Bangladeshi tertiary hospital (2018-2022), 2024 - Present.

Overview

Third-generation cephalosporin resistance among Enterobacterales is a growing concern in wound infections, often forcing clinicians toward carbapenems as a default choice. This project used five years of hospital antibiogram data (2018-2022) to look for hidden co-resistance structure that could reveal carbapenem-sparing alternatives.

Approach

I quantified third-generation cephalosporin resistance patterns in wound Enterobacterales isolates and mapped co-resistance networks across the dataset to identify modules of antibiotics whose resistance patterns cluster together, highlighting carbapenem-sparing treatment options.

My role

I led the study design, data analysis, and manuscript preparation for this project, working with hospital laboratory data spanning 2018-2022.
